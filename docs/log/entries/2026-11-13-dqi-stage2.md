## Question

Where does the public frontier (`e9f54be3`: 892 qubits, 395,332 Toffolis, ratio about 65) get its 2.5x in `Q^2`, 2.2x in
`T` and 1.7x in `R` over our Lanczos decoder (1,403 qubits, 868,770 Toffolis, R about 2.07, ratio 6.81)?

### Qualitative

* Qubit ids 840 to 891 (52 qubits) are live from `extract` to the end with no growth phase: the whole decoder state lives
  in the 70 syndrome words and is transformed in place. Ours holds a growing pair, carried scalars and 74 to 129 scan scratch.
* Hypothesis tested and held: a pivot that is an element of the quadratic extension is zero with probability about 2^-24
  per step, which explains the frontier's zero failures. The classical Cayley map confirms the failure factor of 1.74x.
* The naive quantum version of that idea is a loss (about 1,900 qubits, 2.5M to 3M Toffolis, ratio about 1.6). The
  frontier must use an in-place Schur-type extraction that is not derived here.
* Szego-type halving from `T_{N-i} = conj(T_i)`: no saving found (derived). The conjugate-reverse is orthogonal in a
  high window, not the low one, so no single polynomial replaces the pair.
* Negative result: the "phase-only trace" trick (23 per position) does not help our scan, whose phase against a classical
  mask is already free; the 138 remaining are product 42, norm 18, subfield inversion 25, two products 36, gate 6, zero test 11.
* Reverse is dearer than forward (`unextract` 97k against `extract` 55k), consistent with measurement-based clearing repaid in
  the reverse. Update 2026-11-14: our extract plus synthesis round trip (222,595) is within 0.6% of the public four stages
  (221,174); the mask gauge saves at most 770 Toffolis (0.2%); our full circuit (391,326) is already 1% below 395,586.
* Estimates for the Gu et al. route (dialog-free BM, ratio about 8; with the reciprocal scan about 11) were later built and
  tested in separate entries.

## Decision

The note ends with "decision needed": frontier-class qubits needs the in-place Schur-type algorithm (open research), and a Gu et
al. style BM decoder is a multi-day build with an estimated ratio about 8 (about 11 with the reciprocal scan). No new full-size
circuit was verified in this pass; best own DQI stayed at 6.81. ADR-0009 was amended on 2026-11-13.
