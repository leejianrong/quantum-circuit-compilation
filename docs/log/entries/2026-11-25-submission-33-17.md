## Question

Does the hosted score for the `best/` portfolio promoted on 2026-11-24 match what our own verification predicted (about 33.1)?

## Setup

`bench pack` built `submission.zip` (18 files) from `best/`: invsqrt `bb676b95`, dqi `141f02ac`, dfthcsos `24516e97`. The owner approved the upload. Submitted with `kaggle competitions submit` as ref 58812685 on 2026-11-24, and `bench lb` recorded the leaderboard rows.

## Results

### Quantitative

Public score **33.17031**, against 28.71588 for 58673310 [M]. Our worst-of-10-salt geometric mean was about 33.08, so the hosted figure sits within 0.3% of it. The submission passed with no verifier failure.

### Qualitative

The prediction held. The small gap is expected, since the hosted run uses its own salts. This is the first score above our earlier 28.7 line, and the gain comes entirely from the public package credited in `docs/PROVENANCE.md` (source S19).

## Decision

`best/` stays as it is. The next gain has to come from a new public frontier or from our own search, and the Circuit Explorer plan is the route to see what is inside these circuits.
