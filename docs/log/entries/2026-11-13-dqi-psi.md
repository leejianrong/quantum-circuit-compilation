## Question

Can the Forney scan of our dialog-free BM decoder (96 Toffolis per position, 386k of 741k) be replaced by a scan of about
23 per position, after the published idea of Gu et al. (arXiv:2610.22367 SI I.1)?

## Setup

This entry was backfilled from the study note and git history on 2026-11-15. Apache-2.0 repository stated; nothing copied;
the public DQI lineage has no licence. Classical code only: `src/dqi_gen/bezout.py`, `tests/unit/test_dqi_bezout.py`. No
circuit was built or run. A time-boxed session on the fixed-slot Euclid history is dated 2026-11-13. Commit: not recorded.

## Results

### Quantitative

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| scan per position (zero test 11 + gate 12, phase free) | 23 |
| scan total, 4,024 positions | 93k (against 386k) |
| predicted total if in place: Euclid forward / reverse | about 40k / 35k |
| predicted total: synthesis of `(u, v)` | about 50k |
| predicted overall | about 218k replacing 386k; `T` about 570k, `Q` 1,870 to 1,910, ratio about 10 |
| out-of-place history (36 quotients + 36 swap bits) | 468 extra qubits: `Q^2` +56 to 57% against `T` -23%, a loss |

The classical identity holds in all 200 random trials (weights 1 to 35): the monomial index is always 36 and the cofactors
fit in 35 words, the number of zero syndrome words that in-place Omega leaves [M]. All predicted costs are [I].

### Qualitative

* The step that fails is parking the Euclid history. Which array shrinks depends on `(k, delta)`, so the freed slot is
  data dependent; aligning the windows needs a barrel shift. Estimated cost of parking by selection: over 40k Toffolis, taking
  the reciprocal path from 218k to over 300k for under 10% of `T`.
* The swap bit is ambiguous from the after-state (both branches leave a nonzero constant term and `delta <= 0` after many
  no-swap steps), unlike the BM decoder where the shifted template has a zero constant term. The quotient cannot be recovered
  either: `f_old(0) = c f'(0)` is exactly what the step destroys.
* Other routes were rejected: a second BM run on `O / E` (108 words needed, 71 available) and reuse of the main BM's template
  (the Lanczos-style 138 per position, worse than 96).
* Correction to the BM entry: it projected ratio 11 to 12 for this scan. That held only if an in-place history existed; the
  better estimate with one is about 10, and none is known.

## Decision

Thread closed. The classical Bezout identity stays in `src/dqi_gen/bezout.py` for a future attempt with a published
fixed-slot construction (top-degree Euclid with padding is the one untried formulation).
