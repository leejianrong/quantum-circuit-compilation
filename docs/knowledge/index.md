# Knowledge

What we currently believe, kept separate from the story of how we found out (the [log book](../log/index.md)).

* [Claims register](claims.md): durable statements, whether each holds, was refuted or is unverified, and the log entry that is the evidence.
* [Assumptions](assumptions.md): defaults we took without proof, so a wrong one can be found and corrected.
* [Open questions](open-questions.md): what is not yet known, and who or what decides it.
* [Environment](environment.md): machines, versions, limits and where things live outside git.

Rules: a claim changes status only through a new log entry (a correction), and the register links that entry.
Nothing here is copied from a third-party source; see [PROVENANCE](../PROVENANCE.md).
